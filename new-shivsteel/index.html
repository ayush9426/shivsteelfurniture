Our Work Gallery

Real photos from our fabrication workshop and installation sites showcasing our finish and quality.

Shiv Steel Furniture (SSF) was founded with a single mission: to create steel fabrications that serve generations. We take pride in building heavy-duty swings (julas), designer sofas, premium double beds, and high-safety stair/balcony railings.

We are committed to using the highest grade materials (stainless steel 202, 304, and 316) along with modern fabrication technologies. This allows us to deliver products that exceed client expectations in terms of rust resistance, weld longevity, and surface polish.

Whether you need customized size dimensions to fit a specific bedroom, a rust-proof swing for a garden, or a glass balcony railing system for a commercial project, we handle fabrication from blueprints to safe final installation.
